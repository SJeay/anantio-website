1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Anantio Pvt Ltd ("Anantio", "we", "us", or "our") handles personal information relating to visitors, customers, and users of our websites, services, and ABIZ business management platform.

By using our services, you acknowledge the practices described here. This policy should be read with our Terms of Service.

2. Who We Are

Anantio Pvt Ltd provides websites, design, digital marketing, automation, technical support, and digital products including ABIZ. For questions about this policy or privacy requests, contact our grievance officer at connect@anantio.com.

3. Information We Collect

We collect only information reasonably required to provide, secure, improve, and support our services.

Business dataCustomers, leads, tasks, invoices, notes, and files entered into ABIZDelivering the platform and requested workflows

4. How We Collect Information

Information may be provided directly when you create an account, submit a form, contact us, purchase a service, or upload information to ABIZ. We also collect limited technical information automatically through logs, cookies, and similar technologies. We may receive information from payment processors, analytics providers, and business customers where they are authorized to share it.

5. Why We Collect Information

  • To provide, operate, personalize, and maintain our services.
  • To process payments, manage subscriptions, and send service messages.
  • To answer questions, provide support, and deliver projects.
  • To protect accounts, detect abuse, and maintain platform security.
  • To understand usage, fix errors, and improve products and communication.
  • To meet contractual, legal, tax, and regulatory obligations.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep sessions secure, remember preferences, understand product usage, and measure communication performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling essential cookies may affect sign-in and core features. See our Cookie Policy for details.

7. Third-Party Services

We use carefully selected providers for hosting, authentication, payments, email, analytics, customer support, and infrastructure. These providers may process information only to provide services to us and under contractual or legal confidentiality obligations. Their own privacy policies may also apply.

8. Data Storage Location

Information may be stored and processed in India or in other countries where Anantio or its service providers operate. When information crosses borders, we use reasonable contractual, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the data.

9. Security Measures

We use access controls, encrypted transmission, credential protections, monitoring, backups, and role-based access practices designed to protect information. No internet service is completely secure, and you are responsible for keeping account credentials confidential.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers, professional advisers, payment partners, or authorities where required by law. We may also share information as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or business transfer, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

11. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary for the purpose collected, to provide services, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and satisfy legal or accounting obligations.

Data typeTypical retention
Active account and business dataWhile the account or service is active
Support and project communicationsUp to 3 years after resolution
Billing and tax recordsAs required by applicable law

12. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, portability, restriction, or deletion of personal information, and may object to certain processing or withdraw consent. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Contact us at connect@anantio.com.

13. Data Deletion Process

You may request deletion by emailing us from your registered address with the account or company name. We verify the request, confirm any service consequences, and normally complete deletion within 30 days. Some information may remain where retention is required for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, security, or dispute-resolution purposes. More detail is available in our Data Deletion Policy.

14. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services, laws, or privacy practices change. We will update the date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies.
