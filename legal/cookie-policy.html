1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. Similar technologies such as pixels, local storage, and software development kits may perform related functions. They help websites remember sessions, preferences, and activity.

2. Cookie Types

TypeWhat it doesTypical duration
Essential cookiesSupport sign-in, security, navigation, and core service functions.Session or up to 1 year
Analytics cookiesHelp us understand visits, feature use, errors, and performance.Up to 2 years
Marketing cookiesMeasure campaigns and deliver more relevant communications where enabled.Up to 1 year
Preference cookiesRemember settings such as theme, language, or display choices.Up to 1 year

3. Your Choices

You can disable or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block third-party cookies, delete existing cookies, or ask before accepting a cookie. Blocking essential cookies may prevent sign-in or reduce functionality. Where a consent tool is available, you can update non-essential choices there.

4. Third-Party Tools

Some pages may use third-party providers for analytics, payments, embedded content, or communications. Those providers may set their own cookies subject to their privacy policies. We do not control cookies set by third-party websites you visit through an external link.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our technology or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top indicates when the policy was last revised.
