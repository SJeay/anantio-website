FAQ

Questions about ABIZ.

What is ABIZ?

ABIZ is a WhatsApp Business CRM product developed by Anantio Private Limited for businesses to manage leads, customer conversations, WhatsApp workflows, and support operations.

Who can use ABIZ?

ABIZ is designed for growing businesses, sales teams, customer support teams, agencies, and service providers that use WhatsApp Business for customer communication.

Does ABIZ work with Meta Ads?

Yes. ABIZ is built to help businesses organize leads from Meta Ads and route incoming WhatsApp conversations into a central business dashboard.

How does WhatsApp Cloud API connection work?

Customers connect their WhatsApp Business number and required business assets through ABIZ onboarding and Cloud API setup procedures.

Can I upgrade from Monthly to Lifetime?

Yes. Customers can request plan transition guidance from support and relevant account information can be reviewed for upgrade eligibility.

Do you provide support?

Yes. Support is included with the monthly plan and priority support is available for lifetime customers.
